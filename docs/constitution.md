# GEGH Constitution & Phase 3.0 Specification

## Phase 3.0 Realignment: Mirdita-Primary Empirical Domain
- Mirdita Gheg micro-attestations constitute the primary observational baseline.
- Oral forms are first-class empirical objects and must be captured before normalization.
- Standard Albanian and Tosk serve as comparative and documentary controls, not the baseline.

## GEGH-INV-22 — Dialectal Primacy Without Historical Prejudgment
A designated dialectal research zone may constitute the primary observational corpus without being encoded as historically prior, genealogically ancestral, or linguistically more ancient. Any claim of antiquity, continuity, preservation, or ancestry must be independently evaluated from evidence.

## GOLD-INV-03 — No Source, No Validation
A claim-level validation field may not be PASSED unless the corresponding source/evidence anchor exists and has been inspected.
